Safety tips

Protective gear is essential for every ride

Wear helmet, jacket, gloves, pants and boots.

Always check your motorcycle before ride.

Before every ride mandatory inspect brakes, tires, lights and all fluid levels.

Ride defensively

On motorcycle, you are worse visible than cars.

Always try to predict other drivers behavior and avoid risky maneuvers.

Regular maintenance

You should do regular maintenance work such as fe. oil change. You can use our website for managing maintenance
